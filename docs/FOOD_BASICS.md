# Food Basics monitoring policy

Food Basics is an independently monitored retailer, not merely a comparison source.
Read the registry, runbook and docs/DIGEST.md. Preserve every other retailer, the private
sender/recipient settings and scheduler state. Configuration does not authorize a deal run
or setup email. The supplied market is Kingston, Ontario, with sources in its profile.

## Verify the local flyer

Start with the official flyer and a verified Kingston store selection. The public store
reference at /en/find-a-grocery/100949 is a discovery reference, not an inferred personal
store preference. The generic flyer page may default to another city; inspect actual
region/store applicability. Search current/next-week editions and date ranges, and
corroborate actual pages using independent Ontario archives and local listings beyond
Flipp. An archive title, cached snippet or reused scan does not establish a new edition.

Check exact product/brand, size/count, fresh/frozen status, meat cut/bones, price per lb
versus kg, multi-buy quantity, app/member/coupon terms, limits and item-specific dates.
Never assume personal eligibility for student, targeted or other restricted discounts,
reward balances, coupon stacking or price matching. Keep cash cost separate from points.
Respect private allowed purchase channels without publishing the preference or rationale.
Online grocery prices and pickup services do not by themselves prove local shelf stock.

## Useful shopping coverage

Research all relevant advertised categories; do not artificially restrict the flyer to
food. Compare exact or clearly labelled comparable products with Metro and other local
stores. Modest verified savings and practical everyday purchases belong in the roundup.
Keep brand/size differences explicit; do not equate a lower sticker price with lower unit
cost. Verify per-kg/lb/piece or volume and required quantities. Do not invent travel costs
or publish personal ranking inputs. Apply the compact 8-10 assessed-entry digest contract,
including the section 'Not worth treating as headline bargains' within that same total.

## State and delivery

Use data/food-basics/latest.json and reports/food-basics/, preserving dated edition history.
Earlier Food Basics prices quoted inside another retailer's report are NOT a full Food
Basics monitoring baseline. Do not bootstrap change evidence from those isolated comparisons.
At the first reliable full source inspection, save the new profile's baseline silently;
no first-coverage alert or old-offer backfill. Future mail requires a verified change plus
useful value and per-recipient deduplication. One retailer's email never covers another.
Source failures must not erase a baseline or suppress independent retailer checks.

This profile uses the existing single-profile CLI. It does not bundle a live scraper or
change/value engine; another agent/VM supplies its collector and private state/settings.
Only verified actual cash prices and advertised dates belong in compatible JSON snapshots.
