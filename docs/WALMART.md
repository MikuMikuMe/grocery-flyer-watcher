# Walmart Canada monitoring policy

Apply the runbook and docs/DIGEST.md to the independent Walmart profile. Its configured
market is Kingston, Ontario; store 3043 at the official /en/store/3043 page is a public
local source reference, not personal account or home-store information. All other watches
and private delivery rules remain unchanged.

## Sources, market and advertised-offer scope

Inspect the official selected-store flyer, weekly flyer features and actual linked product
pages. Verify Kingston selection rather than silently using a default city. Corroborate
Ontario flyer images and dates with independent archives/local listings, not just Flipp.
Do not substitute Walmart US, other provinces or a Marketplace seller's price for Walmart
Canada's local advertised offer. Walmart subdomains and mirrored feeds are not independent.

Track weekly flyers and dated event/seasonal catalogues separately. Record all existing
verified editions silently at initialization; encountering an older booklet later is not
new publication. The weekly-features grid and product pages corroborate flyer offers;
this addition does not create an unrestricted Rollback/clearance/product-price watcher.
Unrelated evergreen prices or search-page reorderings are not independent alert triggers.

All advertised categories are eligible, including groceries, cleaning, household goods,
clothing, furniture, electronics and seasonal products. These are examples, not filters.
Read accessible pages without claiming exhaustive coverage when access is partial.

## Check what the price actually buys

Match exact item/SKU/variant, brand, pack, model/configuration, condition and seller.
Distinguish sold/shipped by Walmart from third-party Marketplace sellers, and in-store
cash prices from online-only, delivery or pickup prices. A pickup time displayed without
verified store selection is not evidence of Kingston item availability. Follow private
allowed purchase channels; put important channel/eligibility limitations beside the item.

Verify multi-buy quantities, limits, actual event dates, coupons, subscriptions/member or
account requirements, and any mandatory fees. Do not assume a private offer, stock, waived
fees or credit-card eligibility. Separate immediate cash payable from rewards, financing
instalments and later rebates. Recalculate unit costs from verified package sizes if a
site's unit-price label is inconsistent; never infer a package's mass from a product name.
Food, household and technology comparisons must match relevant quantities/specifications.

Apply the 8-10 assessed offers PER qualifying changed retailer contract. Most should be
useful buys, with modest verified savings welcome. Count weaker comparisons under 'Not
worth treating as headline bargains' within the total; no filler or generic disclaimer.

## Isolated initialization and runtime

Use data/walmart/latest.json and reports/walmart/ with separate private state. Prior
Walmart comparisons in other retailer reports are not an established monitoring baseline.
Initialize silently after actual verification; no setup, initial-coverage or backfill mail.
Future delivery needs a genuine advertised change plus useful value, successful public
publication and private recipient-specific duplicate checks. Preserve other profiles.

The existing CLI accepts config/walmart.toml for dated verified JSON. It does not provide
browser/store selection, a live scraper or the full selection/delivery policy by itself.
No personal account, contacts, settings or delivery state belong in this public profile.
