## Worth buying

5. **Bulk seedless oranges — $1.97/lb ($4.34/kg).** Weight-based, not $1.97 per bag.
6. **Dr. Oetker Ristorante or Casa di Mama selected pizza — $3.98/package.** Selected products; different pizzas vary in weight.
7. **Taylor Farms selected chopped salad kit — $4.44/package.** Convenience format; shorter fresh shelf life.
8. **Selected SunChips or Smartfood popcorn — $3.47/bag.** Package sizes vary.

## Not worth treating as headline bargains

9. **Your Fresh Market medium ground beef, value pack — $6.34/lb ($13.98/kg).** Not price-comparable to a lean beef-and-pork blend; weight and fat content matter.
10. **Royale bathroom tissue, selected pack — $12.97.** Sheet count/ply and physical rolls needed before ranking value; package equivalents can mislead.
