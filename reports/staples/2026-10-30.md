## Best buys

1. **ASUS TUF VG27AQE5A 27-inch 2560×1440 165Hz IPS gaming monitor — $139.** Flyer page 1; same exact model is listed at **$249** on [ASUS Canada's own store](https://www.asus.com/ca-en/displays-desktops/monitors/tuf-gaming/tuf-gaming-vg27aqe5a/) and $169.99 on [Staples' product page](https://www.staples.ca/products/3188847-en-asus-tuf-gaming-27-qhd-15001-03-ms-165hz-ips-gaming-monitor-vg27aqe5a). A useful advertised same-model value if the printed offer is accepted in-store; individual-branch stock and price are unconfirmed.
2. **Expo low-odour dry-erase markers, selected 16-pack — $9.99 ($0.62/marker).** Flyer page 2, **October 30–November 1 only**; chisel or fine-tip selected varieties. [Staples 16-pack chisel-tip listing](https://www.staples.ca/products/592678-en-expo-low-odour-dry-erase-markers-chisel-tip-assorted-colours-16-pack) was $24.99 outside the new flyer context. Useful only if a full 16-pack is needed.
3. **Staples Stickies 3×3-inch yellow adhesive notes, 12-pack — $8.49 ($0.71/pad).** Flyer page 2, **October 30–November 1 only**. Large-pack practical stationery purchase; not comparable to Post-it Super Sticky quality or different pad sizes without checking.
4. **Staples Tech AA alkaline batteries, 36-count reusable case — $12 ($0.33/battery).** Flyer page 1. Non-rechargeable bulk pack, good unit outlay for devices that regularly use AA cells.

## Worth buying

5. **Brother Work Smart MFC-J1260W Wi-Fi colour inkjet all-in-one — $69.** Flyer page 1, item 3135720. Print/copy/scan with LC501 cartridges. [Brother Canada](https://www.brother.ca/en/colour-inkjet-multifunctions/c/pr-printers-colourInkjetMFC) also listed this model on sale for $69.99 plus a $7.25 electronic-handling fee, so Staples' small price advantage is useful but not a unique deep discount. Ongoing ink cost matters; local printer stock unconfirmed.
6. **Bond Street kids' polyester backpack in denim, item 3171589 — $10.** Flyer page 1; compact 11 × 16.25 × 5 inches, padded straps, kids/teens size. [Exact Staples item](https://www.staples.ca/products/3171589-en-bond-street-kids-polyester-backpack-denim) lists the same cash price and a 1-year limited warranty. Buy for the small size, not as a large adult travel bag.
7. **Staples white 20 lb copy paper, letter size, 500 sheets — $6.99 (1.40¢/sheet).** Flyer page 1; useful if printing regularly. [Staples copy paper category](https://www.staples.ca/collections/printer-copy-paper-8454) includes the same advertised 500-sheet paper price. Not the same exact SKU as the previously tracked FSC-certified multiuse paper.
8. **Crayola cube facial tissues, 85 sheets — $1 (1.18¢/sheet).** Flyer page 13, also appeared in the previous week's flyer: **continuing price**, not a new reduction. Practical small purchase where this format is useful.

## Not worth treating as headline bargains

9. **Royale Velour 3-ply facial tissues, 82 sheets per box, 6-box pack — $6.49 ($1.08/box).** Flyer page 3. Shoppers' October 31–November 1 flyer advertises selected six-box facial tissues at $4.99, although sheet count and ply vary. This is not a proven cheapest per-sheet purchase.
10. **Staples Massage Chair black, model 53075-BLK — $199.** Flyer page 1. Includes heat/massage, but its [specifications](https://www.staples.ca/fr/products/3168767-en-staples-massage-chair-black) list fixed-height armrests and no adjustable lumbar support; less compelling as a long-hours ergonomic chair despite the advertised reduction. Branch stock unverified.
