# Food Basics | 2026-11-05 to 2026-11-11

## Best buys
- **Broccoli crowns — $1.44 each**. A strong vegetable buy for everyday meals.
- **Fresh bone-in pork combination chops — $2.22/lb ($4.89/kg)**. Good meat value; account for the bones when comparing edible portions.
- **Red or black seedless grapes — $1.88/lb ($4.14/kg)**. A useful fruit buy; choose a quantity that will be eaten while fresh.
- **Primo pasta (900 g) — $1.68/pack ($1.87/kg)**. Strong pantry value in a large dry-pasta pack.

## Worth buying
- **Seedless navel oranges (3 lb) — $2.98/pack**. Useful bagged fruit for a modest total outlay.
- **Fresh Atlantic salmon fillets family pack — $9.98/lb ($22.00/kg)**. A practical fish option; freeze meal portions that will not be used promptly.
- **Galbani mozzarella (500 g) — $4.98/pack ($9.96/kg)**. Useful cheese value in the larger advertised format.
- **Kraft peanut butter (1 kg) — $4.98/pack ($4.98/kg)**. A sensible pantry purchase when the larger advertised jar is needed.
- **Bakery baguette — $0.99 each**. An inexpensive bread option to use fresh or freeze.

## Not worth treating as headline bargains
- **Kraft Dinner noodle meal (12 x 200 g) — $9.99/pack ($4.16/kg)**. Compared with Metro at $9.99/pack. Useful only when the multipack will be used; not a unique competitive advantage.
