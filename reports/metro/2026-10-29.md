# Metro — Ontario weekly shopping roundup, October 29–November 4, 2026

**Edition:** Ontario weekly flyer, Thursday **October 29** through Wednesday **November 4, 2026**. The edition was verified and first published before its start date; this updated shopping lineup is an **editorial expansion of that same already-handled edition**, **not a new retail offer or another email event**. All listed cash prices are CAD. Kingston branch participation and stock were not independently confirmed by the dynamically inaccessible selected-store viewer; the source-backed claims below are Ontario flyer advertisements.

## Best buys

1. **Fresh boneless, skinless chicken breasts — $4.49/lb ($9.90/kg).** Page 7. Food Basics' matching Ontario October 29–November 4 **boneless skinless breast, family pack** is $6.00/lb ($13.23/kg), a **$1.51/lb** advertised advantage. This Metro price was $4.99/lb in the October 22–28 edition. The packs are not necessarily the same size; compare the actual per-pound shelf ticket.
2. **Divya selected lentils or black chickpeas, 1.81 kg / 4 lb — $3.99 ($2.20/kg).** Page 12. Strong pantry value when the exact variety is useful. **Continuing advertised price**, also present in the prior edition; not described as a fresh price cut.
3. **Black Diamond selected cheese bars (400 g) or shredded cheese (320 g) — $4.99.** Page 1. Approximately **$12.48/kg for a 400 g bar** or **$15.59/kg for 320 g shreds**; compare formats separately rather than treating them as the same weight.

## Worth buying

4. **Belle Grove selected mushrooms, 227 g — $2.44 ($10.75/kg).** Page 1. Useful fresh-produce option when the package and condition match the printed offer.
5. **Blackberries, advertised clamshell — $2.49.** Page 1. Food Basics also advertises $3.00 blackberries that week, but **the two clamshell weights are not established as matching**; do not infer a cheaper per-gram price.
6. **Maggi selected instant noodle packet — $0.44 each.** Page 12. Useful low checkout price for an occasional pantry purchase; exact packet weight/flavour needs matching before unit-price comparisons.
7. **Primo selected canned beans — $1.49 per can.** Page 12. Practical pantry option; can size and variety need checking for a fair $/kg comparison.
8. **Kraft peanut butter, 1 kg — $4.99 ($4.99/kg) with Moi member pricing.** Page 1. **Membership-priced**, not an unconditional price; compare ingredient formulation and regular/non-member price before deciding.

## Not worth treating as headline bargains

9. **Dr. Oetker selected Ristorante/Casa di Mama frozen pizza — $3.49 for Moi members** (regular advertised $4.49). Pages 1 and 14. Conditional on Moi member pricing, and not evidence that every variant is a best local buy.
10. **Campbell's selected condensed soup cans, 284 mL — $0.99 each ($3.49/L).** Page 1. Food Basics advertises selected Campbell's condensed cans at $1.00, so the **one-cent difference** is not a meaningful headline saving; confirm matching flavours and volume.
