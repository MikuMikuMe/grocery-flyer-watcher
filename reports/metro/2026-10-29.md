**October 29–November 4, 2026 | CAD**

## Best buys

1. **Red Grill top sirloin steak, value pack, or roast — $8.99/lb ($19.82/kg).** Prominently advertised on the front page. This beef offer belongs in the shopping shortlist; it was omitted from earlier versions of this report. The steak and roast are alternatives within one advertised offer, not two entries.
2. **Divya selected lentils or black chickpeas, 1.81 kg / 4 lb — $3.99 ($2.20/kg).** Useful shelf-stable pantry value. Continuing advertised price, not a newly claimed price reduction.
3. **Black Diamond selected cheese bars, 400 g, or shredded cheese, 320 g — $4.99.** $12.48/kg for the bar or $15.59/kg for shreds; compare the formats separately.

## Worth buying

4. **Belle Grove selected mushrooms, 227 g — $2.44 ($10.75/kg).** A useful produce purchase in the verified advertised size.
5. **Blackberries, advertised clamshell — $2.49.** Package cash price only: the comparison clamshell weights have not been established as equivalent, so no per-gram advantage is claimed.
6. **Primo selected canned beans — $1.49 per can.** A pantry candidate; the exact can size and variety must be established before claiming a unit-price advantage.
7. **Kraft peanut butter, 1 kg — $4.99 ($4.99/kg), Moi member price.** Membership is required for the advertised price; compare matching formulations.
8. **Fresh skinless chicken breast — $4.49/lb ($9.90/kg).** The flyer says skinless, not boneless. Bone content is not established by that label. No equivalent-cut comparison with boneless breast, or saving against it, is claimed.

## Not worth treating as headline bargains

9. **Dr. Oetker selected Ristorante/Casa di Mama frozen pizza — $3.49 for Moi members** (other advertised price $4.49). A conditional purchase; membership alone does not make the offer poor, but a best-local-price claim has not been established.
