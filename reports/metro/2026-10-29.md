# Metro — Ontario weekly shopping roundup, October 29–November 4, 2026

**October 29–November 4, 2026 | CAD**

## Best buys

1. **Red Grill top sirloin steak, value pack, or roast — $8.99/lb ($19.82/kg).** Front-page beef offer; steak and roast are alternatives within one advertisement, not two separate roundup entries.
2. **Fresh chicken wings or fresh split chicken breast — $3.99/lb ($8.80/kg).** Front-page poultry offer. The wings are a useful batch-cooking/freezer candidate; do not compare bone-in wings or split breasts as equivalent to boneless breast. The two cuts share one advertised offer and one roundup slot.
3. **Divya selected lentils or black chickpeas, 1.81 kg / 4 lb — $3.99 ($2.20/kg).** Useful shelf-stable pantry value. Continuing advertised price, not a newly claimed price reduction.
4. **Black Diamond selected cheese bars, 400 g, or shredded cheese, 320 g — $4.99.** $12.48/kg for the bar or $15.59/kg for shreds; compare the formats separately.

## Not worth treating as headline bargains

A same-cut local price advantage has not been established, so treat it as a comparison/conditional purchase rather than a proven standout bargain. Do not substitute prices for bone-in chops, pork loin, ribs or another week's sirloin promotion.
