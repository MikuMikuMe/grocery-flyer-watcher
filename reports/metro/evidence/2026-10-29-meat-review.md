## Evidence boundaries

An advertised price does not automatically prove an exceptional bargain. Missing evidence
of a standout price is also not evidence that the item was previously evaluated and
rejected: the earlier omission is acknowledged rather than retroactively justified.

Do not infer a local unadvertised markdown from this regional flyer. Do not transfer a
customer-specific discount, account offer or transaction result to public advertised prices.
Do not infer package weight from a receipt total or apply a transaction discount universally.
No receipt, loyalty identifier, purchase history or private offer information is retained
in this record. A later correction to this evidence is editorial and cannot by itself
satisfy the new-advertised-offer notification gate.
